# Human-reviewed source research drafts preserve rule authority

For recognized items in Hanoi or Ho Chi Minh City with no active reviewed rule (including an expired rule), an editor researches one item/city at a time in Sanity; active rules are not monitored or rewritten.
The tool previews claim-linked exact passages from current official sources, requires a current binding legal instrument for disposal decisions (agency guidance may clarify logistics), and checks official in-force status plus amendment/repeal records; unresolved or conflicting evidence yields only a transient gap report, not a claim that no route exists.
Store versioned passages with Sanity citations and migrate runtime lookup from `app/knowledge-base.json`; human review and publication remain mandatory, and published rules alone authorize app guidance.
